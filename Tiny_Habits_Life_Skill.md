# Tiny Habits (Life Skill)

## Question 1

**Question:** In this video, what was the most interesting story or idea for you?

**Answer:** The most interesting idea for me was starting with very small habits instead of making big changes immediately. Small actions are easier to repeat and can slowly become automatic habits.

## Question 2

**Question:** How can you use B = MAP to make making new habits easier? What are M, A and P?

**Answer:** B = MAP means Behavior = Motivation, Ability, and Prompt. Motivation is the desire to act, Ability makes the action easy, and a Prompt reminds us to perform the behavior.

## Question 3

**Question:** Why is it important to "Shine" or Celebrate after each successful completion of a habit?

**Answer:** Celebrating after completing a habit creates a positive feeling and helps the brain remember the behavior. This makes us more likely to repeat the habit and turn it into a routine.

## Question 4

**Question:** In this video, what was the most interesting story or idea for you?

**Answer:** The most interesting idea was that small improvements can create big results over time. Improving by just 1% every day shows that consistency is more important than making huge changes at once.

## Question 5

**Question:** What is the book's perspective about Identity?

**Answer:** Atomic Habits explains that habits help shape our identity. Instead of only focusing on what we want to achieve, we should focus on the person we want to become and build habits that support that identity.

## Question 6

**Question:** Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit easier to do?

**Answer:** A habit can be made easier by reducing the effort needed to perform it. We can prepare things in advance, remove unnecessary steps, and start with a very small version of the habit.

## Question 7

**Question:** Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit harder to do?

**Answer:** A habit can be made harder by increasing the effort or number of steps required. For example, keeping the phone away while studying makes using it less convenient and reduces distractions.

## Question 8

**Question:** Pick one habit that you would like to do more of. What are the steps that you can take to make the cue obvious or the habit more attractive or easy and/or response satisfying?

**Answer:** I would like to read more regularly. I can keep a book where I can see it, start with two pages after dinner, choose an interesting book, and celebrate after completing my reading.

## Question 9

**Question:** Pick one habit that you would like to eliminate or do less of? What are the steps that you can take to make the cue invisible or the process unattractive or hard or the response unsatisfying?

**Answer:** I would like to spend less time on my phone. I can keep it away while studying, turn off unnecessary notifications, set app limits, and avoid using it when I feel bored. This makes the habit invisible, difficult, and less satisfying.
