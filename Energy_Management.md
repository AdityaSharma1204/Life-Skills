# Energy Management

## Question 1: What are the activities you do that make you relax - Calm quadrant?

* I like listening to music.
* I go for a walk when I need a break.
* I spend time with my family and friends.
* I enjoy reading books.
* I spend some time doing my hobbies.
* I practice meditation.
* Sometimes, I simply take some rest.

## Question 2: When do you find getting into the Stress quadrant?

* I feel stressed when I have many tasks to complete.
* Deadlines can make me feel stressed.
* I feel some stress when I am learning something new.
* Intense workouts can also make me feel stressed.
* Difficult tasks can put me in the stress quadrant.

## Question 3: How do you understand if you are in the Excitement quadrant?

* I feel energetic and motivated.
* I become interested in what I am doing.
* I enjoy working on the task.
* I feel focused and involved.
* I feel challenged, but not too overwhelmed.

## Question 4: Paraphrase the Sleep Is Your Superpower video in your own words in brief.

* Sleep gives the brain and body time to recover.
* It helps with learning and remembering things.
* Getting enough sleep helps improve concentration and decision-making.
* Poor sleep can affect our mood and health.
* Good sleep is important for keeping the body and mind healthy.

## Question 5: What are some ideas that you can implement to sleep better?

* I can follow a regular sleep routine.
* I can make sure I get enough sleep.
* I can avoid too much stimulation before going to bed.
* I can keep my sleeping environment comfortable.
* I can exercise regularly.
* I can spend some quiet time relaxing before sleep.

## Question 6: Paraphrase the video - Brain Changing Benefits of Exercise.

* Exercise increases blood flow to the brain.
* It can help improve learning and memory.
* Regular exercise can improve our mood.
* Exercise can help reduce stress.
* It supports better brain health.
* Exercise is good for both mental and physical health.

## Question 7: What are some steps you can take to exercise more?

* I can exercise regularly.
* I can start by taking a walk every day.
* I can include light exercises in my routine.
* I can practice yoga.
* I can play sports that I enjoy.
* I can take short movement breaks during the day.
* I can make exercise a regular part of my daily routine.
