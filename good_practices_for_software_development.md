# Good Practices for Software Development — Review Questions

## Question 1: Which point(s) were new to you?

- **Over-communicating** with the team when there are delays, technical issues, or changes in requirements.
- **Asking clear questions** by explaining the problem, what I tried, and sharing relevant screenshots or code.
- **Being mindful of teammates' time** by combining multiple questions into one message and choosing the right communication medium.

## Question 2: Which area do you need to improve on? What are your ideas to make progress?

I need to improve my **communication and asking questions when I am stuck**.

To improve, I will:
- Clearly explain the problem and mention what I have already tried.
- Ask questions early instead of staying stuck for too long.
- Share relevant code, screenshots, or examples to make the issue easy to understand.
- Keep the team updated about my progress and any blockers.
